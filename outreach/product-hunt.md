> A PH launch = one traffic spike + a **permanent, high-authority profile page** that AI cites
> for "startup marketplace" / "Acquire alternatives". Do it once, do it well.
> ⚠️ Owner schedules & hits publish (needs the PH account + maker profile).

## Pre-launch checklist
- [ ] Maker account with a filled profile (@Dan_white_22 linked).
- [ ] Warm up: follow/upvote a few launches in the days before (PH throttles cold accounts).
- [ ] Assets ready: logo (240×240, use `icon-512.png` downscaled), 3–5 gallery images
      (catalog page, a listing with the MRR chart, the /faq or comparison), optional 30s demo.
- [ ] Pick a **Tuesday–Thursday**, launch at **00:01 PT** (full day of votes).
- [ ] Line up 5–10 people to genuinely check it out on launch day (no vote-begging in comments).

## Description (PH "description" field)
Startup Market is a free catalog of startups and SaaS businesses for sale where each listing's
revenue is read directly from the seller's payment provider — Stripe, LemonSqueezy or Polar — via a
read-only key. No screenshots: you see real MRR, 30-day revenue, growth and price history across
7,000+ listings in 7 languages. It's a catalog, not a broker — free to browse, free to list, deals
happen directly between buyer and seller. Metrics are informational.

## Reply snippets (keep handy for comments)
- **"How is this different from Acquire/Flippa?"** → Revenue is read live from the payment provider
  rather than self-reported, and it stays a free informational catalog instead of a broker that
  closes deals on-platform. Different approach; verify and compare for yourself.
- **"Is my Stripe data safe?"** → It's a **read-only** key — it can read metrics, not move money,
  refund, or change anything. Used only for the numbers on the listing.
- **"Do you take a cut?"** → No. Free to browse and list; deals happen directly, no commission.
