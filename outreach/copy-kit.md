## Long description (≈600–800 chars)
Startup Market (startupmarket.tech) helps buyers find startups and SaaS businesses for sale with revenue they can actually trust. Instead of relying on screenshots or seller claims, each listing's metrics are read directly from the seller's payment provider — Stripe, LemonSqueezy or Polar — through a read-only connection. Buyers see current MRR, trailing 30-day revenue, growth rate, asking price and revenue-history charts across a catalog of 7,000+ startups in dozens of categories, in seven languages.

It's free to browse and free to list. Startup Market is an informational catalog, not a broker or escrow service: it never moves money or takes a commission — buyers and sellers negotiate and settle directly. All metrics are shown as the provider's API returns them and are informational; independent due diligence is recommended before any transaction.
